# 031: Seasonal sign-in scene

Status: in-progress

Try a seasonal scene on the sign-in page in the prototypes: a background image per season in
light and dark, a weather effect, and an intro on the first visit. The draft images and the
snow and intro mock-up are in `design/backgrounds/`. Winter came first; the other seasons get
their own weather effect next. The palette may need a look across the app afterwards, since a
scene may want a deeper dark and a brighter light base than today's tokens.

`prototypes/README.md` ("Seasonal scene and intro") describes the current prototype.
